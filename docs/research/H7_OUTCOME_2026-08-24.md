## 2. Falsification Conditions (F1–F4)

### F1 — Sharp refutation of the "state loss tracks upturn" reading
> `h = 7` loses recurrent states **and** `C_7 < C_6`.

**Verdict: NOT TRIGGERED.**
The computation found that h=7 does lose recurrent states (valid = 37,698, essential = 32,976), and `C_7 ≈ 1.109441`, which is strictly greater than `C_6 ≈ 0.996661`.

### F2 — The structural assumption breaks
> `essential(L_7) = valid(L_7)`, i.e. no state loss at `h = 7`.

**Verdict: NOT TRIGGERED.**
The computation certified strict state loss (essential < valid).

### F3 — Hypothesis failure
> Multiple dominant SCCs, period `> 1`, or lattice covolume `> 1` at `h = 7`.

**Verdict: NOT TRIGGERED.**
The essential graph has a unique dominant SCC, a certified period of 1, and a lattice determinant of ±1.

### F4 — Prediction miss without structural failure
> Structural hypotheses hold, `C_7 > C_6`, but `C_7` falls outside `[1.15, 1.40]`.

**Verdict: TRIGGERED.**
The structural hypotheses hold perfectly, and `C_7 > C_6` holds. However, `C_7 ≈ 1.109441` falls strictly below the preregistered interval [1.15, 1.40]. This explicitly falsifies the quantitative extrapolation used to predict the band, while preserving the structural phenomenon (state loss tracks upturn).

## 3. Evidence Status / Known Gap

The h=7 evaluations performed in this run rely strictly on spectral bounds and Parry-measure thermodynamic limits. **No independent finite-n validation of the collision ratio R_n^{(7)} was performed.** This is a documented evidence gap, analogous to the finite-n confirmation gap already present for h=6. The present h=7 closure is recorded as a preregistered computational result, but it is not promoted into the published certified h=2,...,6 family in this branch. Any extension of the published theorem/certified family to h=7 requires a separate evidence/certificate and manuscript review.
