## 1. Falsification Conditions (Verbatim from Preregistration)

### F1 — Sharp refutation of the "state loss tracks upturn" reading
> \h = 7\ loses recurrent states **and** \C_7 < C_6\.

**Verdict: NOT TRIGGERED.**
The computation found that h=7 does lose recurrent states (valid = 37,698, essential = 32,976), but C_7 \approx 1.109441, which is strictly greater than C_6 \approx 0.996661. Therefore, state loss and collision-constant increase still coincide in direction.

### F2 — The structural assumption breaks
> \essential(L_7) = valid(L_7)\, i.e. no state loss at \h = 7\.

**Verdict: NOT TRIGGERED.**
The computation certified strict state loss (essential < valid).

### F3 — Hypothesis failure
> Multiple dominant SCCs, period \> 1\, or lattice covolume \> 1\ at \h = 7\.

**Verdict: NOT TRIGGERED.**
The essential graph has a unique dominant SCC, a certified period of 1 (coprime walk lengths 15 and 16 at the root), and a lattice determinant of \pm 1 (from equal-length L=19 cycles at the root).

### F4 — Prediction miss without structural failure
> Structural hypotheses hold, \C_7 > C_6\, but \C_7\ falls outside \[1.15, 1.40]\.

**Verdict: TRIGGERED.**
The structural hypotheses hold perfectly, and C_7 > C_6 holds. However, C_7 \approx 1.109441 falls strictly below the preregistered interval [1.15, 1.40]. This explicitly falsifies the quantitative extrapolation used to predict the band, while preserving the structural phenomenon (state loss tracks upturn).
