## 7. Exact full-shift local-composition lemma

Consider the full ternary shift with a tilt \(t\) favoring letter \(a\):

\[
p_a(t)=\frac{e^t}{e^t+2},
\qquad
p_b(t)=p_c(t)=\frac1{e^t+2}.
\]

For a canonical half-Parikh profile \(v=(v_1,v_2,v_3)\), let \(E_v\) be the event that two consecutive length-h halves have equal Parikh vector lying in the S3 orbit of \(v\).

Let

\[
q_v(t)=\mathbb P_t(E_v).
\]

If \(M_v=h!/(v_1!v_2!v_3!)\), then

\[
q_v(t)=
M_v^2\,
\frac{\sum_{c\in \operatorname{Orb}(v)}e^{2c_a t}}
{(e^t+2)^{2h}}.
\]

At \(t=0\),

\[
q_v'(0)=0
\]

and

\[
\frac{q_v''(0)}{q_v(0)}
=
\frac49(3B(v)-h)
=
-\frac49S(v).
\]

Therefore, if occurrences of \(E_v\) receive an infinitesimal soft penalty \(e^{-\varepsilon}\), the pressure identity

\[
\partial_\varepsilon P(t,\varepsilon)|_{\varepsilon=0}
=
-q_v(t)
\]

gives the exact full-shift infinitesimal variance response

\[
\boxed{
\partial_\varepsilon a_v(0)
=
-\;q_v''(0)
=
\frac49\,q_v(0)\,S(v)
}.
\]

So \(S=h-3B\) is not merely a fitted empirical scalar: it is the exact quadratic local-composition driver in the unconstrained/full-shift soft model.

### Cubic invariant

At the next tilt order,

\[
(\log q_v)'''(0)=8J(v)-\frac{4h}{27}.
\]

Thus:

- \(B\) / \(S\) enters at quadratic order, exactly where variance lives;
- the cubic S3 invariant \(J\) first appears one tilt order later.

This strongly suggests that \(J\) is not part of the leading local variance term, though it may enter nonlinear or dynamical corrections.

## 8. Critical Diophantine locus

The condition \(S=0\) is equivalent to

\[
(v_1-v_2)^2+(v_2-v_3)^2+(v_3-v_1)^2=h.
\]

Writing

\[
x=v_1-v_2,\qquad y=v_2-v_3,\qquad v_3=c,
\]

gives

\[
2(x^2+xy+y^2)=3c+x+2y.
\]

The first nonzero integer solutions are:

- h=2: (1,1,0)
- h=6: (3,2,1)
- h=8: (4,2,2)
- h=14: (6,5,3)
