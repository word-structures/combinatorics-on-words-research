# Paper 8 scope and provisional paper skeleton

## Distinction from Paper 7

**Paper 7:** can an Abelian-square-free word be infinitely extendable to the right but not embeddable in any bi-infinite Abelian-square-free word?

**Paper 8:** how does forbidding a particular Abelian-square Parikh profile change the statistical dynamics—especially asymptotic letter-count variance—of the background subshift?

## Provisional title

**Parikh Geometry, Symmetry, Return Kernels, and Variance Response in Abelian-Square Avoiding Shifts**

Alternative shorter title:

**Profile Geometry and Variance Response in Abelian-Square Avoiding Shifts**

## Provisional narrative

1. A finite profile-response pattern motivates the question but is not the theorem.
2. Full-shift computation exposes the quadratic B/S invariant.
3. Constrained backgrounds replace h/3 by a dynamic block-variance threshold B_c.
4. S3 symmetry separates Perron growth from letter-imbalance susceptibility.
5. Profile deletion is a low-rank defect; a finite return kernel controls the Perron branch.
6. Rigorous soft-to-hard response is reduced to a finite-dimensional certified problem.
7. H8 serves as a worked discovery/certification laboratory.

## Manuscript section skeleton

1. Introduction and motivating response phenomenon

10. Discussion and open problems

## What the paper must not become

- a claim that 19/19 proves a universal minimum-B law;
- an H8-only table paper;
- a novelty claim based on AI recollection rather than literature audit;
- a black-box numerical result without replay/certification boundaries.
