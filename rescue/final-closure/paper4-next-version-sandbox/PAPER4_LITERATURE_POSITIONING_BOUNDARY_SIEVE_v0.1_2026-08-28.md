## Main conclusion

The new prefix-state projection has a plausible project-specific formulation, but several ingredients are clearly close to prior art. Do not claim novelty yet.

## Closest prior-art layers

### 1. Template parents and boundary Parikh corrections

Currie–Rampersad and especially Rao–Rosenfeld already formulate parent-template equations as an incidence-matrix contribution plus Parikh vectors of prefixes/suffixes at morphism boundaries. The Paper-4 boundary equation is therefore best presented as a concrete partial-assignment specialization of the template-parent algebra, not as the invention of boundary correction.

### 2. Modern template sieving

Eyidoğan–Göral–Tanısalı (2026 preprint) develop a sieve for the template method specifically to eliminate parent/ancestor candidates before expensive realization checks. Their paper makes “early exact structural filtering” close prior art. What appears narrower and potentially project-specific here is the staged unassigned-role projection on a fixed macro cover and its compilation into forbidden prefix-Parikh states.

### 3. Prefix-Parikh / automatic methods

Rigo–Stipulanti–Whiteland study effective automata for abelian complexity in Parikh-collinear morphic fixed points. Other automatic-word work constructs automata from prefix-defect/prefix-Parikh functions to recognize abelian-square occurrences in particular sequences. These are conceptual neighbors of a prefix-state decision system.

### 4. Parikh automata / semilinear constraints

Parikh automata are a broader formal-language framework in which finite automata are combined with arithmetic constraints on letter-count vectors. The present fixed-length DAG is simpler and more concrete, but literature on Parikh automata and semilinear Parikh images should be checked before making methodological novelty claims.

## Important caution

Do not claim that Abelian-square occurrence is generally Walnut/first-order decidable for automatic words. Existing Walnut material explicitly notes that this fails in general; successful automata constructions concern special sequences/properties.

## Safe novelty target

A precise claim to audit is:

“Given a staged uniform block construction over a fixed finite macro-factor cover, if an unassigned role occurs once in that cover, all long-period Abelian-square constraints above the block length project exactly to order-independent failures or forbidden prefix-Parikh states of that role, giving a finite exact pre-certifier.”

No source checked in this round was found stating exactly this role-staged single-occurrence projection. That is NOT evidence of novelty; it is only the right specialist-search target.
