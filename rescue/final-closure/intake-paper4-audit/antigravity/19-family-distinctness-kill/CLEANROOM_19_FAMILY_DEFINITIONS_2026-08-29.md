# Phase 1: Clean-room 19-Family Definitions

**Date:** 2026-08-29

## 1. Domain Equations

The six geometric domains define the allowed depth tuples `(u,v,w)`.
The variables satisfy `0 <= u, v, w <= L-1`.

Full unrestricted domains:
- **Z**: `u + w = 2v`
- **P**: `u + w = 2v - L`
- **M**: `u + w = 2v + L`

Truncated (block-coincident) domains, parameterized by `a = i_0` and `r >= 2`:
- **Z_s**: `(a, a+r, a+2r)` with `a >= 0`, `a+2r <= L-1`
- **P_t**: `(a, a+r, a+2r-L)` with `a+r <= L-1`, `a+2r >= L`
- **M_t**: `(a, a+r-L, a+2r-L)` with `a+r >= L`, `a+2r <= 2L-1`

## 2. Reduced Support Map and Endpoint Convention

For a tuple `(u,v,w)` and a role mask `(chi_0, chi_1, chi_2) in {0,1}^3`, the raw signature is:
  `sigma_raw = chi_0*x_u - 2*chi_1*x_v + chi_2*x_w`

The reduction map `red` performs the following operations:
1. **Endpoint convention**: Set `x_0 = 0` (delete any term with depth 0).
2. **Combine**: Sum coefficients of identical depths.
3. **Filter**: Remove any term with a coefficient of 0.

## 3. The 19 Families

A family is defined as the COMPLETE SET of reduced signatures generated by applying a specific mask over a specific domain. The 19 stable families are:

1. **E**: Empty family (generated by mask 000 over any domain).

2. **Zs-A**: `Z_s` with mask 111.

3. **Z-O**: `Z` with mask 001 or 100.
4. **Z-C**: `Z` with mask 010.
5. **Z-M**: `Z` with mask 011 or 110.
6. **Z-OO**: `Z` with mask 101.
7. **Z-A**: `Z` with mask 111.

8. **P-O**: `P` with mask 001 or 100 (and `P_t:001`).
9. **P-C**: `P` with mask 010.
10. **P-M**: `P` with mask 011 or 110.
11. **P-OO**: `P` with mask 101.
12. **P-A**: `P` with mask 111 (and `P_t:111`).
13. **Pt-M**: `P_t` with mask 110.

14. **M-O**: `M` with mask 001 or 100 (and `M_t:100`).
15. **M-C**: `M` with mask 010.
16. **M-M**: `M` with mask 011 or 110.
17. **M-OO**: `M` with mask 101.
18. **M-A**: `M` with mask 111 (and `M_t:111`).
19. **Mt-M**: `M_t` with mask 011.

There are exactly 19 objects. The names (e.g. Z-O, P-M) correspond to the complete sets of signatures, not merely the applied masks.
