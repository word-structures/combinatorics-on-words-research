# Literature Matrix

## 1. Gabriele Fici, Svetlana Puzynina (2023)
**Title:** Abelian combinatorics on words: A survey
**Venue:** Computer Science Review
**Access Level:** ABSTRACT_ONLY
**Relevant Result:** Summarizes known results in abelian combinatorics.
**What this DOES establish:** Provides the comprehensive background for abelian combinatorics on words up to 2022.
**What this DOES NOT establish:** Does not establish statistical or variance properties of abelian square-free languages using thermodynamic formalism.
**Overlap:** STANDARD_BACKGROUND

## 2. D. A. Lind (1989)
**Title:** Perturbations of Shifts of Finite Type
**Venue:** SIAM Journal on Discrete Mathematics
**Access Level:** ABSTRACT_ONLY
**Relevant Result:** Establishes bounds on the change in entropy when a subshift of finite type is perturbed by forbidding a finite word.
**What this DOES establish:** Foundational bounds for entropy drop under structural perturbations (forbidding words).
**What this DOES NOT establish:** Does not study the asymptotic variance of additive observables (like letter frequency), nor does it classify forbidden words by their Parikh profile.
**Overlap:** KNOWN_METHOD

## 3. Nick Ramsey (2024)
**Title:** Entropy bounds for multi-word perturbations of subshifts
**Venue:** Ergodic Theory and Dynamical Systems
**Access Level:** ABSTRACT_ONLY
**Relevant Result:** Establishes a general criterion for upper bounds on entropy difference.
**What this DOES establish:** General bounds for entropy drop when multiple cylinders are deleted.
**What this DOES NOT establish:** Does not address asymptotic variance, Green-Kubo formalisms, or Parikh-composition specific responses.
**Overlap:** CLOSE_RELATED_RESULT

## 4. Haritha Cheriyath, Nikita Agarwal (2021+)
**Title:** Subshifts of Finite Type with a Hole / Escape Rate with Markov Measure
**Venue:** Various
**Access Level:** ABSTRACT_ONLY
**Relevant Result:** Escape rate depends on the correlation polynomial and overlap structure of the forbidden words.
**What this DOES establish:** That the combinatorial overlap structure of a hole affects the escape rate (eigenvalue drop) exactly.
**What this DOES NOT establish:** Does not study the variance response of an observable, or relate it to the Parikh profile imbalance of the hole.
**Overlap:** CLOSE_RELATED_RESULT
