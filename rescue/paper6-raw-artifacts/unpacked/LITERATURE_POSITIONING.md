# Literature and project positioning — preliminary

This is not a novelty closure.

## Direct aa2f context

Veikko Keränen's public 2026 research page states that in the ternary case
single-letter (period-1) abelian squares are allowed while all other abelian
squares are to be avoided.  It also lists:

- long aa2f and aa2fr words;
- a length-40 aa2f collection with simultaneous extension experiments;
- exact counting data for ternary aa2f words at finite lengths.

The public project poster uses the same aa2f definition and defines aa2fr as
aa2f plus the six FORBID4 factors.

## Existing project result that must not be reinvented

The project's knowledge state already records a proved Fekete upper bound

    aa2f global growth rate <= 1.9915.

Therefore "the aa2f language has an exponential growth rate" and "use Fekete
to upper-bound it" are not new Paper-6 contributions.

## Classical surrounding theory

For factorial/power-free languages, entropy from submultiplicative word counts
is standard.  Richard & Grimm use precisely this principle for ordinary
ternary square-free words.

Finite-memory forbidden-word languages are shifts of finite type; their entropy
is the logarithm of a Perron spectral radius.  Decreasing finite-type
approximations to a subshift and monotone entropy convergence are classical
symbolic-dynamics facts.

## What was not located in this preliminary pass

No source was located that directly studies the exponential survival rate of
concatenations drawn from a *fixed selected library of aa2f/aa2fr blocks* of
length 40, conditioned on remaining globally aa2f/aa2fr.

Search failure is not proof of novelty.
