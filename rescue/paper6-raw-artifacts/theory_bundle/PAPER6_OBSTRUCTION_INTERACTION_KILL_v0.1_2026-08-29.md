# PAPER 6 — OBSTRUCTION INTERACTION KILL TEST v0.1
**Date:** 2026-08-29  
**Status:** structural diagnostic; not a manuscript

## Question

Can the exact total future-count space be explained as a low-degree linear
feature space of the raw affine obstruction coordinates
\(\Delta_{k,j}\) (plus the short literal suffix needed for FORBID4)?

For a state \(s\), treat each boundary-obstruction coordinate as a categorical
observable and form indicator functions for:

1. one coordinate at a time;
2. pairs of coordinates;
3. if needed, higher interactions.

Then test whether the exact Krylov vectors

\[
\mathbf 1,\quad M\mathbf1,\quad M^2\mathbf1,\ldots
\]

lie in the resulting feature span.

## Result

The naive low-degree-feature explanation fails.

### BAL3 L4, Kmax=6

- raw states: 210
- exact unary Krylov rank: 7
- degree-1 obstruction feature rank mod \(1{,}000{,}003\): 122
- degree-1 feature space contains only the constant Krylov vector
- after adding all degree-2 coordinate interactions, feature rank becomes 210,
  i.e. the full raw state space

So degree two contains the counting space only because it reconstructs the
entire literal state space.

### INTERIOR L5, Kmax=5

- raw states: 438
- exact unary Krylov rank: 27
- degree-1 feature rank: 240
- degree-1 contains only the constant Krylov vector
- degree-2 feature rank: 438, again the entire raw state space

### HASH30 L4, Kmax=5

- raw states: 323
- exact unary Krylov rank: 47
- degree-1 feature rank: 174
- degree-2 feature rank: 321

The degree-2 space is again essentially the whole literal state space.

## Interpretation

The small exact future-count space is **not** explained by simply taking a
low-degree polynomial expansion of the individual raw obstruction coordinates.

This is an important negative result.

The obstruction coordinates are useful physical coordinates, but arbitrary
pairwise interactions rapidly recover almost all literal information.

Therefore the sought compression must use the **dynamics/action of the
obstructions**, not merely a static feature expansion.

The next natural object is the cyclic/invariant future space generated by the
transfer operator itself.

## Caveat

The feature ranks in this diagnostic were computed over a finite field and are
being used only as a kill test. They are not promoted to manuscript-level
rational-rank claims.
