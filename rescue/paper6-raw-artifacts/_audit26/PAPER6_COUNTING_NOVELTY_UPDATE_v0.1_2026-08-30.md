# PAPER 6 — COUNTING NOVELTY UPDATE v0.1
**Date:** 2026-08-30  
**Status:** literature-boundary note

## New literature checkpoint

A targeted search confirms that counting repetition-avoiding words and proving
exponential growth are established research themes.

In particular:

- James Currie proved exponential growth for binary words avoiding Abelian
  fourth powers (Theoretical Computer Science 319, 2004).
- Currie, Mol, Rampersad and Shallit, *Extending Dekking's Construction of an
  Infinite Binary Word Avoiding Abelian 4-Powers*, SIAM Journal on Discrete
  Mathematics 38(4), 2024, give an additive-power decision method for certain
  morphic sequences and improve a counting lower bound to
  \(\Omega(1.172^n)\).
- Entropy of ordinary square-free languages also has a classical literature.

## Consequence

Paper 6 must not frame its novelty as:

> “we count avoiding words”,

or:

> “we attach entropy to a repetition-free language.”

The novelty candidate is narrower and more specific:

> **relative weighted survival inside an externally selected equal-length block
> library, with exact local Abelian geometry compiled into finite cut-profile
> defect templates and long-range dynamics handled as a weighted decorated
> additive process.**

This distinction should be kept explicit in all future manuscript drafts.

## Strongest open novelty target

Classical template papers are primarily about morphic avoidance/decision.

The present program asks for a reusable weighted counting operator for an
arbitrary selected block language and for its asymptotic survival rate.

That selected-library weighted-template synthesis is the part that now
deserves the deepest literature audit.
