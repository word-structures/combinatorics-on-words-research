
# Phase A: Exact Re-derivation of O1 term
**Date:** 2026-08-29

## 1. O1 Matrix and t-derivatives
Let $\mathcal{B}_v(z,t) = I + z O_1(t) + \dots$
The relative weight of a letter is $w_c(t) = e^{t f(c) - P_{base}(t)}$, where $f(c) = \delta_{cc} - 1/3$.
At $t=0$, $w_c(0) = 1/3$, $w_c'(0) = (\delta_{cc}-1/3)^2 - 2/9$. Total curvature $\sum_k w_{c, tt}^{(k)}(0) = 0$.
The entries of $O_1(t)$ are $O_1(W, W') = z \cdot w_{x_{2h}}(t)$.

## 2. Profile-Orbit Averaging
The scalar function is $\phi_v(z,t) = \ell^T \mathcal{B}_v^{-1} m_v(t)$.
Averaging conventions: $\ell^T = \frac{1}{N_v} \mathbf{1}^T$.
The target weight is $m_W(t) = \prod_{i} w_{W_i}(t)$.
The unperturbed sum $\ell^T m_v(0) = 1 \times (1/3)^{2h}$.

## 3. Second Derivatives
From $\phi_{tt} \approx \ell^T m_{tt} - z \ell^T O_{1,tt} m - 2z \ell^T O_{1,t} m_t$:
The $I$ term evaluates to $\sum_k \ell^T m_{tt}^{(k)} = (1/3)^{2h} [ 4 B(v) ]$.
The $O_1$ term involves strings of length $2h+1$. Its total relative curvature is evaluated as:
$$ V_1 = (1/3)^{2h+1} \left( d_1(v)(4B(v) - 8h/3) + \frac{4 P_3}{h^2} \right) $$

## 4. Normalization and the 1/3 Component
From the F5 identity, $\eta_v = - \frac{z^2}{D_v F_z} \phi_{tt}$.
If we isolate the $d_1(v)$ contribution from the cross terms, we substitute $d_1(v) = 1/3 + B(v)/h^2$.
The profile-independent component is $(1/3) \times (-8h/3) = -8h/9$.
This constant is expected to be absorbed into the baseline variance normalization.

## 5. Exact Discrepancy (STOP)
The explicit $O_1$ linear term coefficient of $B(v)$ in $\phi_{tt}$ generated by $V_1$ is $4/h^2$, augmented by cross terms.
When normalized by $D_v$, the coefficient does NOT universally collapse to $-4/3$. The strict scalar expansion leaves cross-terms dependent on $P_3$ and $h$ (e.g., $4 P_3/h^2$).
**Discrepancy:** Under the exact $F(z,t) = z - 1 - z^3 \phi_v$ convention, the $O_1$ operator produces an $h$-dependent rational polynomial that does NOT identically equal $-4/3 B(v)$.
