## Analysis & Promotion

While we successfully discovered candidates with profound **asymmetry** (e.g., `abacabadc`, which has absolutely no valid left-extensions but survives boundlessly on the right), the surviving side invariably undergoes unstructured, strictly monotonic exponential branching. 

We checked for:
1. **C1 (Forced Corridors):** The surviving trees do not funnel into forced deterministic paths. (Even $w_K$'s left side eventually explodes at $d > 20$).
2. **C4 (Morphic-Tail Affinity):** Testing against certified infinite constructions ($g_{85}, g_{98}, g_{109}$) under all alphabet permutations yielded only random-chance alignments (17-21 characters max). 
3. **C5 (Recursive Block Structure):** No repetitive exact substitutions were observed in the greedy DFS branches.

Because none of the surviving trees exhibit a stable deterministic transformation, low-branching infinite nesting, or an alignment to an explicit morphic proof, they offer exactly the same profile as $w_K$: bounded survival evidence without an infinity-certificate signal.
