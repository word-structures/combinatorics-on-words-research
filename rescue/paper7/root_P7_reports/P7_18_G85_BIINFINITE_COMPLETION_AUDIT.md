# P7_18 — BI-INFINITE COMPLETION AUDIT FOR `ad + G85`

We investigated whether the exceptional boundary word $Y = ad \cdot g_{85}^\omega(a)$ admits a rigorously provable left-infinite Abelian-square-free completion $ZY \in \mathcal{A}_4$.

## 1. Exact Predecessor-Boundary Formulation
To construct a bi-infinite completion using the P7_16 residual/morphic machinery, the completion $ZY$ would need to be compatible with $g_{85}$ desubstitution. By Mossé’s recognizability theorem, the right-infinite tail $X = g_{85}^\omega(a)$ has a unique parsing into $g_{85}$ blocks starting precisely at index 0. 

For $ZY = Z \cdot ad \cdot X$ to belong to a morphic predecessor chain, the left extension $Z \cdot ad$ must perfectly align with the block boundaries of $X$. This strictly requires that $Z \cdot ad$ decomposes into a sequence of complete $g_{85}$ blocks, meaning the final block $g_{85}(c_{-1})$ must end with the sequence `ad` (or the boundary between the final block and $X$ must form `ad`).

## 2. Sufficiency of P7_16 States
The P7_16 predecessor machinery is **mathematically incompatible** with a left-completion of this boundary. We performed an exact audit of the $g_{85}$ block suffixes:
* $g_{85}(a)$ ends with `ca`
* $g_{85}(b)$ ends with `db`
* $g_{85}(c)$ ends with `ac`
* $g_{85}(d)$ ends with `bd`

None of the blocks end with `ad`. Furthermore, because $X$ starts with the letter `a`, any cross-boundary junction between a left block and $X$ takes the form $c_{last} \cdot a$, which can never equal `ad`. Thus, `ad` cannot be produced by any aligned desubstitution of $g_{85}$. 

## 3. Predecessor Transition Graph
The predecessor transition graph is **empty** (0 valid states). The boundary configuration `ad | X` cannot be desubstituted into any $B_{-1} | X_{-1}$ under $g_{85}$.

## 4. Recursive/Morphic Backward-Chain Candidates
Because the boundary $ad$ forces $Y$ strictly outside the standard factor language of the $g_{85}$ subshift, no backward-chain candidates exist within the $g_{85}$ orbit. Any bi-infinite completion $Z$ would have to be a non-morphic "hybrid" sequence or generated by an entirely different substitution mechanism not aligned with the right tail.
