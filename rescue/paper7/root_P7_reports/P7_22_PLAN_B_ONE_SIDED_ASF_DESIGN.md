## A. Lessons Extracted from P7_08–P7_21

Our extensive testing of the $B \cdot g_{85}^\omega(a)$ paradigm revealed why finite exceptional boundaries on recurrent cores fail to mathematically certify $w \notin e(\mathcal{A}_4)$:
1.  **Local Defects Vanish Globally:** A finite boundary $B$ is a strictly local perturbation. The right-tail $g_{85}^\omega(a)$ provides an infinite, uniformly recurrent "safe" context. As balanced depth increases, unstructured (non-morphic) left-tails can absorb the local boundary discrepancy and synchronize with the unbounded diversity of the right core.
2.  **Morphic Quotients are Scope-Limited:** Exact desubstitution graphs (like P7_16) only classify left-extensions generated by $g_{85}$ (Scope A). They are blind to the unstructured, non-morphic left completions (Scope B) that empirically survive and expand exponentially in our searches.
3.  **Symmetry of Recurrence:** Any single finite defect leaves the remaining infinite word structurally symmetric (recurrent) at all scales $> |B|$. A bi-infinite completion only needs to solve the boundary once; after that, it can ride the natural bi-extendability of the core.

## B. Formal Design Requirements

To rigorously achieve $w \in re(\mathcal{A}_4) \setminus e(\mathcal{A}_4)$, the Plan B construction must satisfy:
*   **R1 (Right-Infinity by Induction):** The word must be generated by a recursive rule $W_{n+1} = F(W_n)$ where $F$ is mathematically proven to preserve ASF. 
*   **R2 (Global Non-Recurrence):** The left boundary must be structurally unique, anchoring an aperiodic signature that never repeats internally, breaking uniform recurrence permanently.
*   **R3 (Infinite Ladder of Obstructions / Anti-Bi-Extension Invariant):** The construction must inject Parikh discrepancies or parsing obstructions at scales $L_n \to \infty$. A bi-infinite completion would be forced to supply an infinite sequence of exact compensating structures, forcing an arithmetic/Parikh contradiction at some finite scale.
