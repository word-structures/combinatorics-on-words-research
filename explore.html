Explore

Try it. Change it. See the structure.

Patterns become easier to understand when you can manipulate them. Build a word, watch a search, or turn a sequence of letters into something you can see.

Four ways to explore

Pick one. Each opens straight into the tool — nothing to set up, nothing to install.

  1. 01

    Try a word

    Build with a, b and c, one letter at a time, and see what kinds of repeated structure appear as the word grows.

  2. 02

    Watch the search

    Watch a search branch through possible words, backing away when the next step creates a pattern it is trying to avoid.

  3. 03

    See the structure

    Turn a sequence of letters into a path you can follow with your eye. Or compare two words side by side.

  4. 04

    Play

    A puzzle built on the same idea: fill in the gaps while avoiding a particular kind of repeated structure.
